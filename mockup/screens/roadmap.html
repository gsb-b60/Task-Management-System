Same underlying rows, different views
  • A card dropped on the board updates the board immediately.
  • The roadmap re-reads its own key only when its sort differs — no full re-layout, no stale axis.
  • Switching back to the board shows the moved card in its new column, because the board's key was the one invalidated.
